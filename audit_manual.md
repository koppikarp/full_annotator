# Manual annotator audit and shared analysis contract

Audited 2026-09-28. The runnable project entry point is `run_app.py`, which imports the root `popcrop/annotation/app.py`. At audit time, `annotation_app_2.1` contained a separate historical package. The root application and automated pipeline now call the same `popcrop/annotation/analysis.py` implementation. The obsolete application copies were subsequently removed during workspace cleanup; unique archived annotations are preserved in `data/annotation_history`, and retained evidence is listed in `reports/workspace_cleanup.json`.

## What the annotator actually measures

`extract_channel_frame` supports several image layouts. The supplied file is a 16-bit RGB composite stack with shape (13, 5340, 5998, 3), rather than the grayscale-page layout its name might suggest. Frame zero supplies far-red from its red component and reference DAPI from blue; frames 1–12 supply sequencing red, green and DAPI from their respective RGB components. For an alternative grayscale-page stack, the reader instead expects two cycle-zero far-red/DAPI pages followed by DAPI, green, red and far-red pages per sequencing cycle. Display channels and analytical inputs come from the same file values; only the display copy passes through the requested LUT minimum, maximum, colour map and DAPI-overlay settings. Files exported as scaled RGB images would already have altered values before this application receives them. The new provenance therefore records source dtype and layout as well as source SHA256.

Coordinates come first from an image-hash coordinate profile, then an explicitly supplied/default coordinate table, then automatic DAPI detection. That last detector smooths DAPI with sigma 2, thresholds with Otsu, finds distance-transform peaks, and watersheds. This coordinate-generation segmentation does not provide the analytical nuclear mask. Cellpose-related helper functions in app.py are also not the active crop-analysis path.

For each cell and sequencing cycle, the crop half-width is max(24, round(diameter * 1.6 / 2)). The cell coordinate is rounded to an integer pixel before cropping. Current-cycle DAPI is used for local anchoring if its range exceeds 10 counts, otherwise cycle-zero DAPI is tried. Otsu, hole filling and removal of objects smaller than 80 pixels identify nearby DAPI objects. The nearest object's centroid replaces the starting coordinate only when its displacement is within the requested circle radius. The analytical ROI is a circle centred there, with radius max(8, diameter/2), clipped by the crop and image edges. It is not a measured biological nuclear outline. The default is now 55 pixels, configurable through the UI and launcher; the UI now respects `--diameter`.

Green and red are detected independently. Each channel is smoothed with sigma 1 for detection only. The local background is the median of the lowest 40% of smoothed values inside the circular ROI, and the noise is 1.4826 times their median absolute deviation, floored at 0.001. It is therefore neither the whole slide background nor simply the median of all nuclear pixels. The raw-value positive z map uses that background/noise pair.

The strongest smoothed intranuclear peak must pass all of the following gates: peak excess at least 3500 raw counts in green and 650 in red; SNR at least min(requested high threshold, min_snr), whose shared default is 4; nuclear 95th-percentile excess at least 400 counts; and peak/background fold at least 2. The browser historically passed a high threshold of 4, despite the bare endpoint previously defaulting to 3.5. These defaults are now consistent. The named low/hysteresis threshold is accepted for compatibility but is not used by this detector, which does not actually perform hysteresis segmentation. Raising the high slider beyond min_snr=4 does not further tighten the SNR gate. This existing detector behaviour is preserved and documented rather than silently changing historical labels.

A passing peak is delineated at max(background + 0.28 * peak_excess, background + 3 * noise). The connected component containing that peak is retained; a highest-energy-component fallback exists. A one-pixel closing is applied, then the mask is clipped to the ROI. Accepted channel areas are 6 pixels through 60% of ROI area. Thus neither 50–250 nor 50–350 pixels is a hard biological or algorithmic size range. The two accepted masks are combined by union. At most one primary component per channel is retained, and separated green and red components can occur within one cell.

## Features, labels and what was being lost

The base caller extracts each channel's distribution inside its own detected mask. If that channel has no mask but the other one does, its values are measured in the union mask, which is then the other channel's mask. If neither channel has a detected blob, both distributions use the entire circular ROI. Thus a detector-negative channel still has measured raw intensities and can be rescued by the supervised model. The `green_has_blob` and `red_has_blob` flags describe whether the channel was evaluated on a blob/union region, not necessarily whether its own detector was positive. The separately named `green_detected` and `red_detected` are the detector decisions.

For each channel the extractor produces min/max, P10/P25/P50/P75/P90/P95, mean, standard deviation, IQR, P50–P90 trimmed mean, P75 upper-core median, background median outside its evaluation mask, median excess, integrated positive excess, a 32-bin histogram and up to 64 representative raw pixel samples. It also produces red/green median and trimmed-mean ratios. These are raw image values or explicitly derived local contrasts, never LUT values. Histograms span the local minimum and maximum; their bin edges are essential and are preserved.

The blob feature extractor measures union area, fraction of the circular ROI, energy-map quantiles/sum/variance-derived smoothness, shape, nuclear-edge distance and saturated fraction. Its contrast and integrated-excess names refer to the derived z/energy map; they are not simply detector counts. Its additional raw-channel background/noise features recompute the lower-40% estimator on unsmoothed pixels, which differs from the smoothed detector baseline by design. Its `p_real_blob` is a hand-written score, not an empirically calibrated probability. `persistence_cycles` defaults to 1 for every manual example and therefore supplies no learned temporal information.

The old browser POST and backend row schema retained only part of the available information. Examples include omitted representative samples, several quantiles and background statistics, channel areas on base rows, and raw photometric/spatial fields on blob rows. Missing backend fields were often filled with zero. The updated UI sends a server-issued analysis token and the server saves the complete shared extractor output itself, so the saved feature values match the exact image/settings shown and cannot be replaced by stale browser feature values. Tokens are bound to image hash, cell, cycle, exact unrounded settings and coordinate context. Expired or mismatched crop tokens are rejected. Older direct API clients remain supported but are explicitly marked `legacy_client_unverified`, without invented analysis settings.

Base labels are A for green only, T for red only, C for both and G for neither. N labels are retained as annotation records but must be excluded from supervised base training and accepted barcode results. There is one final barcode per nucleus, as the user confirmed.

Blob buttons describe correctness of the displayed detector decision. The correct conversion for each channel is `true_presence = (detected == called_correctly)`. For example, 'both correct' plus both detector-negative means no real signal; 'neither correct' plus both detector-negative means both real signals were missed. `call_status != 'neither'` is therefore wrong. The new saved `green_true_presence` and `red_true_presence` preserve the explicit biological targets, and `is_real_blob` is their OR. The annotation list now reconstructs this same meaning instead of presenting correctness as presence. All 16 combinations of two detector decisions and four correctness labels are covered by tests.

Annotation persistence also now keys replacement by source image as well as cell/cycle and well/tile, avoiding loss when a second image reuses cell IDs. Saves use an atomic replacement under a lock. A CSV read error fails the write instead of silently replacing all prior labels with one row.

## What can be reconstructed from historical files

At audit time the root blob CSV contains 381 rows, while the base CSV contains 1776 rows: 794 G, 417 A, 304 C, 245 T and 16 N. The plan conflates the base-annotation count with blob training examples. In the blob CSV there are 324 nonzero areas, 193 greater than 300 pixels and 80 greater than 600 pixels, with maximum 1280. The median area of nonzero entries is 368.5 pixels, so the claimed 158-pixel median is not supported by this particular saved blob table.

For entries with nonzero fraction and area, area/fraction has median 2375.19 pixels and its equivalent-circle diameter has median 54.993 pixels. The 10th and 90th percentiles of the inferred diameter are approximately 50.007 and 55.683 pixels, with maximum 90.534. These support prevalent 50/55-pixel sampling circles, not universal use of the old 90-pixel default. However, fraction was rounded to 0.001. Area/fraction therefore reconstructs an approximate ROI area, not an exact diameter, and provides no diameter at all for empty masks. It also cannot recover centring, crop truncation, changed detector thresholds or the original biological nuclear outline. Perimeter does not repair these missing variables. New records directly store exact requested diameter, pixel ROI area, crop bounds, input and anchored centres, detector settings, radius scale, analysis version, source image hash and coordinate-context digest.

Using explicit detector decisions and correctness labels in the current CSV gives 172 cycle examples with real signal and 209 without. Its existing `is_real_blob` column disagrees on 154 rows. Original label files are retained; semantic repair belongs in a derived training dataset.

The retained `.bak` has 324 rows and includes a contrast of 1,304,035, while the current CSV's maximum median contrast is 271.85. A large z score alone is not proof of corruption: the detector's 0.001 noise floor can produce enormous z scores on flat or quantized backgrounds. Exclusion requires examining the raw crop or failure mechanism. Blindly removing a row because a derived z feature exceeds raw camera range is dimensionally invalid.

The phrase 'intrinsic intensity features' does not make diameter irrelevant. Changing the ROI changes background samples, noise, which peak is eligible, the 95th percentile, mask area and intensity populations used for base features. Removing just area and fractional occupancy cannot eliminate this domain shift. Regenerating features from known raw coordinates under an explicit shared contract is more defensible, with held-out manual-label validation and explicit uncertainty for unrecoverable historical settings. Similarly, a 39.7-pixel equivalent diameter from Otsu-segmented biological masks is not evidence that the historical 55-pixel analysis circle was incorrectly recorded; the two masks measure different things.

## Verified changes and limitations

`analysis.py` now owns the per-cycle raw ROI contract, and app.py delegates to it. It also computes spatial fields consistently from the anchored ROI centre and current-cycle DAPI, preserving missing neighbour context as missing rather than a fabricated feature. LUT changes reuse the same cached analysis and change only rendered images. Cache keys retain full numeric precision. The cache remains capped at 300 crops, and uncompressed TIFFs are memory-mapped read-only instead of copying the complete multigigabyte file into RAM.

`tests/test_shared_analysis.py` contains 23 passing tests: direct detector/feature agreement, UI versus batch feature equality, LUT invariance, exact server-side provenance, all correctness combinations, diameter sensitivity, invalid settings, safe missing-neighbour JSON and isolation of images sharing cell IDs. A targeted run with existing manual numerical tests finished with 29 passed, one absent-fixture skip and five integration tests deselected. The root agent runs the broader pipeline validation separately.

This audit verifies the mechanical manual contract. It does not establish complete true-spot recall for the image, validate the population nuclear count, or guarantee that every automated barcode is within one mismatch. The manual training examples are selected rather than a random image-wide census. A held-out/manual review of detector-negative, low-confidence and mapping-conflict nuclei remains necessary to quantify those claims. Shared features and corrected semantics remove known mismatches; they do not turn the whitelist into ground truth or justify forcing unmatched sequences into it.
